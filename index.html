Hi, I'm Abdirahman

I'm 23 years old, live in Horsens, and study Software Technology Engineering at VIA University College. I enjoy learning how to build things with technology and bringing people together.

My story

I am originally from Somalia and have also lived in Egypt and Turkey. Experiencing different places and languages has helped me connect with people from different backgrounds.

What matters to me

Working with young people is especially important to me. I enjoy helping children feel included, try new activities, and make friends. That is why I have taken part in youth camps and community activities.

I am also vice president of Somalisk Studenterorganisation (SSO) in Odense, where students can meet, share experiences, and support one another. My work with SSO, volunteer trips, and local events has shown me how much people can achieve together.

What I bring

My studies have taught me to think through problems, while working and volunteering have taught me to listen, explain things clearly, and stay calm when plans change. I want to keep developing both my technical and people skills.
